How to play

3 to 15 players. Everyone holds six picture cards.

  1. Each round one player is the storyteller. They pick a card from their hand and give a clue for it — a word, a phrase, a sound, a song lyric, anything.
  2. Everyone else secretly plays a card of their own that could also fit that clue, hoping to be mistaken for the original.
  3. The cards are shuffled and laid face up. Everyone except the storyteller votes for the one they think was the storyteller's. You can't vote for your own.
  4. Scoring
    • Everyone found it, or nobody did → storyteller scores 0, everyone else scores 2.
    • Otherwise → the storyteller and each correct guesser score 3.
    • Either way, you score 1 for every vote your own card attracted.
  5. The storyteller passes to the next player, hands are topped back up to six, and it all happens again.
  6. First to 30 points wins.

The whole game is in that scoring. Too obvious and everyone guesses right — you score nothing. Too cryptic and nobody does — you score nothing. Aim for a clue that some will get.

With exactly 3 players, everyone except the storyteller plays two cards, so there are enough decoys to vote between.

Reverie is an independent, open-source clone of Dixit, the storytelling card game by Jean-Louis Roubira and Libellud. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by them — if you enjoy this, buy the real thing.
