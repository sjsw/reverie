## Card artwork

The deck is public-domain artwork supplied under the open-access programmes of
the **Art Institute of Chicago** and **The Metropolitan Museum of Art**. Only
works those institutions have explicitly flagged as public domain are used.

The images are not stored here — `public/cards/manifest.json` records each
work's title, artist, date, holding institution and source URL, and
`npm run restore-deck` fetches them. The same attribution is shown in the app
on the **See the deck** screen.
